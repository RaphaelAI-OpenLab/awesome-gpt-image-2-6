## Rights and validation limits

All files use source-attributed status and exact original source credits. Public availability and attribution are not permission. No separate redistribution license was established, and the repository MIT license does not cover third-party media. No explicit redistribution prohibition was found in the retained source content; this was not a comprehensive or current terms review and is not a legal conclusion.

Every file's SHA-256, byte length, actual format and complete decode were checked. All static/reference images, all 278 GIF frames and all 40 MP4 frames were visually inspected. The 47 files total 19,926,553 bytes. No file exceeds 10 MiB. No GPS EXIF metadata was found in the inspected still/GIF files. Raw source captures, prompt transcriptions and private QA derivatives are not published.

IDs, exact media URLs and SHA-256 hashes were checked against the 129-case base and within the increment, with no duplicates. Similar scenes/alternate settings remain grouped as workflows. Heuristic perceptual similarity is not proof against every crop, edit or repost.

Orylia's six recovered draft workflows and 13 media files remain excluded: the primary gallery/run-manifest capture needed to recheck exact model and prompt attribution was not recovered. Secondary audit notes and file names alone do not close this gap. Its historical failed access is not a finding about reuse rights, and was not retried.

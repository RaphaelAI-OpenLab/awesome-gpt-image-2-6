## Verification and reuse limits

Exact media URLs and SHA-256 values are unique within this batch and absent from the 108-case base dataset. All stills, references and animated frames were visually inspected. No EXIF/XMP payload was exposed by the decoder. Visual inspection and duplicate checks do not independently prove the stated model or rule out every repost or crop.

No explicit redistribution license was verified. Attribution, public availability and customer-output commercial-use terms do not license these site samples. The repository MIT license does not cover third-party media. Current availability and current terms were not retested. No copied prompt text, raw source-page capture or derived QA image is published in this batch.

Their license to X does not establish an independent repository redistribution license. Section 4 restricts reproduction and distribution outside X's permitted interfaces, terms and licenses, and restricts unauthorized scraping.
- [Pixexid license](https://pixexid.com/license) and [terms](https://pixexid.com/terms): the site states that images published publicly on Pixexid use CC BY 4.0. A matching Pixexid image page for this X-only comparison was not established, so this repository does not apply that license to the two previews.
- No independent redistribution permission was verified for these four specific works. No creator-specific prohibition was established either; an absent prohibition does not supply permission. Attribution and a public URL are not a license.
